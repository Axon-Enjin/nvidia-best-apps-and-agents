SentinelClaim v1 · internal prototype format

This format lets you test the complete review loop independently. It is not an official PhilHealth CF4, CF5, or eClaims schema.

Draft claim · XML

<SentinelClaim version="1">
  <ClaimId>SYN-1042</ClaimId>
  <PatientId>SYN-P042</PatientId>
  <AdmissionDate>2026-10-12</AdmissionDate>
  <DischargeDate>2026-10-17</DischargeDate>
  <Diagnosis code="J18.9">Pneumonia</Diagnosis>
  <ClaimAmount>18000.00</ClaimAmount>
</SentinelClaim>

Chart · UTF-8 text or text-based PDF

Claim ID: SYN-1042
Patient ID: SYN-P042
Admission date: 2026-10-12
Discharge date: 2026-10-17
Diagnosis code: J18.9

Clinical narrative and supporting observations…

The Patient ID line is required to match the chart. Explicit dates and diagnosis codes are compared when present. Local checks do not interpret a narrative diagnosis. Optional Nemotron review compares the narrative and must quote both sources.
